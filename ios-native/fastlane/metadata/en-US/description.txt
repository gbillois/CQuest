Master French verb conjugation through an epic fantasy platformer adventure!

ConjugationQuest turns the challenge of learning French conjugation into a thrilling side-scrolling quest. Jump, run, and battle your way through 5 beautifully crafted biomes — Forest, Desert, Mountain, Snow, and Desolation — while conjugating verbs to defeat enemies and unlock gates.

HOW IT WORKS
• Encounter enemies and conjugation gates as you progress through each level
• Answer French conjugation questions correctly to defeat foes and advance
• Practice all major tenses: Présent, Passé composé, Imparfait, Futur, and Conditionnel
• Conjugate across all six pronouns (je, tu, il/elle, nous, vous, ils/elles)
• Cover all three verb groups: regular -er, -ir, and irregular verbs

CHOOSE YOUR HERO
Pick from 8 unique characters — Paladin, Ninja, Pirate, Mage, Barbarian, Cat Warrior, Golem, and Knight — each with their own projectile attacks. Unlock new heroes in the in-game shop using gold earned through gameplay.

ADVENTURE AWAITS
• 5 themed worlds with procedurally generated levels for endless replayability
• Boss battle against a fearsome Dragon with timed conjugation challenges
• Crumbling platforms, moving obstacles, bonus blocks, and tower dungeons
• Three difficulty modes: Easy, Normal, and Chaotic
• Leaderboard to track your scores and compete

DESIGNED FOR LEARNERS
Whether you are a beginner or brushing up on your French, ConjugationQuest adapts to your level. The game supports both English and French interfaces, making it accessible for learners worldwide.

No internet connection required — play and learn anywhere, anytime.
